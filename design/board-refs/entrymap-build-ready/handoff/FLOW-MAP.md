# Flow map

## Universal public flow

1. A new person sees the product promise and may sign in or continue as a guest.
2. Location is explained before the operating-system prompt. “Not now” keeps the map usable with manual area search.
3. Needs are optional, editable, and stored locally for guests. They change match halos, never trust pins.
4. The map appears before any profile requirement. A pin opens the entrance sheet; every chip or badge opens its evidence receipt.
5. Filters cover needs, visually observed features, freshness, distance, and open status. Trust tier is never present as a facet.
6. A correction keeps the existing claim visible while the suggestion is reviewed.

## Community scan flow

1. The primer asks for camera access only after explaining the seven-second scan.
2. The camera guides the full doorway and approach; capture begins only when the person chooses the shutter.
3. Processing reports real events: doorway framed, entry path visible, entrance features checked, and photo quality checked.
4. The person reviews visually confirmed chips before publishing.
5. The Scanned on-site pin lands at 64 px, bounces once, ripples once, and settles to its street size. Reduced motion shows the end state immediately.
6. Offline publication becomes “Saved for later” and uploads when the connection returns.

## Re-check and correction flow

- Staleness speaks about time: “Last checked 11 months ago” or “scanned 14 months ago — help us re-check.”
- Re-check opens the same camera path and adds independent evidence; it does not erase prior receipts.
- Suggesting a correction accepts structured category, plain-language note, and optional photo. Completion reads “Suggestion sent.”

## Owner flow

1. Owner tools live in Profile under “For business owners.”
2. The owner finds the exact entrance before providing business role and contact method.
3. Claim review has visible submitted, business-match, and ownership-review stages.
4. Approval opens the owner workspace for that entrance; it does not create a different consumer map.
5. Owners update observations and photos. Public/state records remain read-only, and community evidence remains independently visible.
6. Publishing produces an owner update receipt. The public pin becomes Owner-confirmed only through the fixed tier rule and displays the storefront plus outlined check—never a shield or “verified.”

`FLOW-MATRIX.csv` is the implementation source of truth for individual actions, destinations, transitions, analytics events, recovery, and reduced-motion behavior.
